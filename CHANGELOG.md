## v0.8.2 · 2026-11-20 · Beta

Fixes from playing v0.8.1: a clear look at your specimens, and a note when the game saves.

### Changes

- Inspecting a dish on the shelf lifts its lid up and out of the way, so nothing sits between you and the specimen. It goes back on when you're done.
- "Saved" shows for a moment beside the version in the corner every time the lab is saved, including the autosave every 2 minutes, so you know it did.

### Fixes

- A plump or big culture no longer sinks half into the agar. Its lower half used to show through the gel with a weird amber tint; now it rests on top, in the dish on the bench and on the shelf.

## v0.8.1 · 2026-11-20 · Beta

Fixes: ten small bugs from the read-through, and hands that react to the work.

### Highlights

- Hands that react:
  - Pressing the syringe's plunger in, your hand gives a little push toward the needle.
  - Squeezing the tweezers hard makes your hand shiver, more the harder you press. You feel the squeeze getting too hard before the sample is crushed.

### Fixes

- A sample dish thrown in the biohazard bin no longer turns solid white.
- Aiming the tweezers at a sample dropped on the bench no longer opens the sample dish's lid.
- An Iridescent culture now shimmers in the dish, not only in the report.
- A specimen no longer lurches when you start inspecting it on the shelf.
- A fast mouse swipe at the bench stops at the edge of the view, instead of overshooting and springing back.
- The radio and the room sound carry on through a long stutter, instead of starting over.
- Spores, sink drops and other little effects no longer pile up while the game is paused.
- A report shelved or thrown away while it's still printing goes as a whole page.
- Pressing Esc right after clicking Settings no longer opens the settings over the running game.
- A sample in the tweezers stays held through a pause, until you press the left button again.

## v0.8.0 · 2026-11-19 · Beta

The front door: a Dutch door with a serving hatch that you open and shut by hand, a walk in through it at the start of every game, and the shed from outside.

### Highlights

- A front door in the shed's front wall: a pine Dutch door, with spear-tipped hinges, ledges and a brace. Its top half opens on its own as a serving hatch over a counter, where buyers will be handed their specimens later; its bottom half opens the whole door.
- It swings like a real door. Pushed open, it carries on and slows to a stop. Shutting, it gathers speed, knocks into the frame and bounces back a little. It has its own sounds: a wooden creak as it swings, and a thud and a latch click as it shuts.
- You work it by hand. Step up to it, press E, and your hand takes it from your side:
  - the counter's edge, the fingers laid over it;
  - the hatch's iron pull from inside, or its knob from the room.

  The hand draws it a little way and lets it swing on. If you're standing where it swings, you step back out of its way. From further than your hand can reach, it shows just its name, and E does nothing.
- Every game starts outside. You step up to the door, push it open with your palm, and walk in round to the stool while it swings shut behind you. Then you sit down at the bench. Any key skips it.
- The shed from outside:
  - a felt roof with an overhang, plank gables, barge boards, a ridge cap and corner boards;
  - a stepping-stone path from the gate, and a doormat;
  - a painted LIFE CREATION sign over the door, and an OPEN card on the hatch;
  - a lamp that lights up at night;
  - a rain barrel with a gutter and a downpipe.

### Changes

- The shed is 60 cm deeper toward the door, so there's room between the stool and the open door.
- The window over the sink is boarded up: old planks are nailed across a smaller opening, and sunlight comes through the gaps in thin streaks. Outside, it has a biohazard sticker.
- The front wall is one piece around the door, its boards lined up inside and out.

### Fixes

- The petri dishes no longer flicker. The agar stops short of the glass, and the labels sit off the wall.

## v0.7.1 · 2026-11-19 · Beta

Hands: a new gloved hand that holds every tool the way a real hand would, and dishes that look like the real thing.

### Highlights

- A new glove: light-blue nitrile with a soft sheen, creases between the fingers and at the knuckles, and a sleeve that fades out smoothly instead of in grainy dots. The hand swings a little with each step when you walk.
- Every tool has a new grip, posed from reference photos of real hands:
  - the tweezers in a pencil grip, the fingers closing as you squeeze;
  - the syringe pushed with the thumb, and drawn with the same hand;
  - the bleach, now a lab wash bottle, held round its body and squeezed;
  - an alcohol wipe, now a folded pad, pinched when you carry it and pressed under your fingertips when you scrub, dragging and wrinkling;
  - the waste beaker wrapped like a drinking glass;
  - the sample dish held by its sides, the thumb on one wall and the middle finger tucked under it.
- Pouring the waste beaker into the bin: it stays in your hand, tips over the open bin and pours a stream into the bag, then tips back. The beaker is smaller (three quarters of its old size), its spout is on the side it pours from, and its label faces you.
- The sample dish is a real 9 cm petri dish: thin walls, a thin layer of honey-colored agar that creeps up the wall at its edge, a see-through blue lid, a label, and 5 samples. The spare dishes in the fridge match.
- The specimen dish gets the same look, with pale honey agar over the cutting mat and a tall, flat-topped see-through blue lid.

### Changes

- The wipes tub hands the next wipe up, and only says "Put back" while you hold a wipe over it.
- Holding right-click leans in a little less, and points the tool in your hand where you look.
- Picking something up no longer flings your hand up.

### Fixes

- The key pictures in the hints (E, Shift, the mouse) could be drawn as a jumble of broken pieces; they show properly again.

## v0.7.0 · 2026-11-17 · Beta

Your lab remembers: quit, come back, and everything is where you left it.

### Highlights

- The game saves your lab: the specimens on the shelf and their reports, the dish you are working on (with its doses so far, or finished with its report by the printer), the sample dish, where the tools are, what's in the syringe, the waste beaker and the bin, spills and mold, the dish number, your best grade and the time of day. When you start the game, you carry on where you left off.
- It saves by itself every 2 minutes, after every dish and when you quit.
- The pause menu's buttons all work now: "Save game" saves with a little chime, "Load game" goes back to your last save, "New lab" starts over, and "Save and quit" saves before the game closes. Load game and New lab ask for a second click first.

### Changes

- Saves from this version keep loading in later versions. A save the game can't read, or one from a newer version, is never written over: it is kept beside the new one, and a note says so.
- The save is a file called `lab.json`, in `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\FDRK Group\LifeCreation` on Windows.
- Not kept: used wipes, what was thrown in the bin (only how full it is), and where you stand: you start on the stool.

## v0.6.0 · 2026-11-17 · Beta

A tidy-up round after a read-through of the whole game: fixes, small touches that make the bench feel more natural, and a clear-out of things the game no longer uses. The lab monitor also gets a pixel font.

### Highlights

- The lab monitor's desktop is lettered in a pixel font, Pixelify Sans, and its counter is labelled "Mold".
- A stray key press can't throw your work away any more: hold <kbd>R</kbd> for a fresh dish, hold <kbd>X</kbd> on the shelf to let a specimen go, and "Reset to defaults" in the settings asks for a second click.
- When the incubation finishes, a note names your new organism and its grade. The culture pulses while it incubates, and the confetti pops.

### Changes

- The hint at the bottom follows what you are doing: "Dish lid · <kbd>E</kbd> Put it back" while you carry the lid, "Incubating…" while the dish incubates, and a seeded dish says it needs a dose first.
- The clipboard, the printed report and the hints show the keys you chose in the settings.
- The how-to-play card explains the lid and the monitor, opens a little quicker, and plays the menu's whoosh.
- A vial's cork stays out a little longer when you look away, so it no longer pops in and out between two draws.
- The download is a little smaller: menu art, models and old code the game no longer uses are gone.

### Fixes

- The lid can't go on a dish that hasn't been dosed; the game says to dose it first.
- A cheering specimen no longer spins into a blur; it turns the way its temperament says.
- The lid and the tool in your hand keep up with you when you walk.
- Rebinding can no longer put two actions on one key: if the swap has nowhere to go, the key stays as it was and a warning sounds. Switching settings tabs while the game waits for a key stops the wait.
- A full syringe, or one holding another reagent, no longer pulls a vial's cork.
- <kbd>R</kbd> on an empty dish says the dish is still empty and leaves the bench alone.

## v0.5.3 · 2026-11-15 · Beta

You can run and crouch now, rebind every key, and lean much further toward what you reach for. Plus fixes from playing v0.5.2.

### Highlights

- Run: hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while standing, 3.2 m/s against 1.8 walking. The waste beaker sloshes harder as you run.

- Rebind any key: in Settings > Controls, click a key and press the new one. ### Changes

- Steady hands only works sitting down, and only with the syringe and the tweezers; standing you just run. It has its own row in Settings > Controls, separate from Run, and the two can share a key.
- The sample dish's full shadow shows on the wall, not just its samples.
- Tools can't be set down inside the sample dish or through other things, so the tweezers can no longer get stuck in it. The placement preview glides from place to place.
- The tweezers lie flat on the bench with the curved part up.
- The sink and the alcohol wipes are back within easy reach, so you no longer have to stand too close to the sink to clean the syringe.
- Drawing from a vial, its name panel steps aside and only the other vials' names stay.
- The key rows in Settings > Controls fade in with the rest of the page.
- After pointing at a specimen, your hand eases away to your shoulder in one smooth move instead of stopping at several points, and two sudden jumps as it came in are gone.

## v0.4.0 · 2026-11-13 · Beta
